A day at a time

Reading plans

A plan is a sequence of days and a few chapters in each. Nothing signs you up and nothing expires — the ticks are kept in this browser, so you can start one, leave it for a month and pick it up where you were.

Christianity

The Bible in a year The whole Christian Bible — Old Testament, the deuterocanonical books and New Testament — in the Douay–Rheims translation, read side by side: two or three chapters of the Old Testament and a chapter of the New each day for a year. 365 days · 1334 readings The New Testament in ninety days All twenty-seven books in the Douay–Rheims translation, about three chapters a day. Judaism

The Hebrew Bible in a year Torah, Prophets and Writings in the Jewish order, two or three chapters a day for a year. The Jewish Publication Society translation of 1917 wherever the archive holds it; the Douay–Rheims fills the books it does not yet. 365 days · 936 readings The Torah The five books of Moses in the Jewish Publication Society translation of 1917, a chapter a day. 187 days · 187 readings The Mishnah, two chapters a day All six orders in the traditional sequence, from Berakhot to Oktzin, two chapters a day. The archive does not yet hold every chapter of every tractate; the plan reads the ones it has. 166 days · 331 readings Josephus, the Antiquities of the Jews The first ten books of Josephus — from the creation to the revolt under Nero — Whiston’s old translation, a section a day. 137 days · 137 readings Josephus, The War of the Jews The fall of Jerusalem told by the man who saw it, Whiston’s translation, a section a day. 119 days · 119 readings The Targums of Onkelos and Jonathan The Aramaic renderings the synagogue read aloud beside the Hebrew: Onkelos on the Torah and Jonathan on the Prophets, two sections a day. 186 days · 371 readings The Psalms in a month Five psalms a day for thirty days, the way the Book of Common Prayer and the siddur have both divided them. 30 days · 150 readings Genesis Fifty chapters in fifty days, in the Jewish Publication Society translation. 50 days · 50 readings The Former Prophets Joshua, Judges, Samuel and Kings — the history from the Jordan to the exile — two chapters a day in the Jewish Publication Society translation. 74 days · 147 readings Isaiah Sixty-six chapters in thirty-three days, in the Jewish Publication Society translation. 33 days · 66 readings A psalm a day All hundred and fifty, one a day for five months, in the Jewish Publication Society translation. The slowest plan here and the one most people finish. 150 days · 150 readings Proverbs in a month Thirty-one chapters, thirty-one days: the old habit of reading the chapter that matches the day of the month. 31 days · 31 readings The Five Scrolls Song of Songs, Ruth, Lamentations, Ecclesiastes and Esther — the Megillot read at Passover, Shavuot, the Ninth of Av, Sukkot and Purim — a chapter a day. 45 days · 45 readings Chronicles The history told again from the priests’ side, two chapters a day in the Jewish Publication Society translation. 33 days · 65 readings Ezra and Nehemiah The return from Babylon, a chapter a day. 23 days · 23 readings The Book of Jubilees Genesis and half of Exodus retold by an angel, two chapters a day. 25 days · 50 readings The midrash on the Five Scrolls Shir HaShirim Rabbah, Ruth Rabbah, Eikhah Rabbah, Kohelet Rabbah and Esther Rabbah, two sections a day. 7 days · 13 readings Pirkei DeRabbi Eliezer The retelling of Genesis and Exodus attributed to Rabbi Eliezer, a chapter a day. 54 days · 54 readings Philo on the Torah Four of Philo of Alexandria’s treatises — On the Creation, On Abraham, On Joseph, On the Decalogue — four short sections a day. 46 days · 184 readings Josephus, The War of the Jews The eyewitness history of the revolt and the fall of Jerusalem, two sections a day. 55 days · 110 readings Maimonides, The Book of Knowledge The first book of the Mishneh Torah — the foundations of the Torah, character, Torah study, idolatry and repentance — a chapter a day. 46 days · 46 readings The Sefer HaChinukh in a year All six hundred and thirteen commandments, in the order of the Torah, with the thirteenth-century explanation of each — one or two a day for a year. 365 days · 613 readings Duties of the Heart Bahya ibn Paquda’s eleventh-century guide to the inner life, in the abridgement, a section a day. 46 days · 46 readings Three medieval philosophers Halevi’s Kuzari, Maimonides’ Eight Chapters and Ibn Gabirol’s Improvement of the Moral Qualities, a chapter a day. 33 days · 33 readings The Nineteen Letters Samson Raphael Hirsch’s defence of Judaism to a doubting young man, a letter a day. 19 days · 19 readings A month of mussar The short ethical classics: Cordovero’s Palm Tree of Deborah, Rabbeinu Yonah’s Gates of Repentance, the Ways of the Righteous, and the letters of Nahmanides, the Rosh and the Vilna Gaon, a chapter a day. 18 days · 18 readings First steps in Kabbalah Sefer Yetzirah, the Bahir, the Heikhalot, the Etz Chaim and the Gate of Reincarnations, a chapter a day — the chapters the archive holds. 7 days · 7 readings Songs of the synagogue Lekha Dodi, Yedid Nefesh, Akdamut, Unetaneh Tokef, Hallel and Ibn Gabirol’s Keter Malkhut, two short pieces a day. 22 days · 43 readings The Ethics of Judaism Lazarus’ four-part moral system in the nineteenth century’s most serious attempt to state Jewish ethics, a section a day. 137 days · 137 readings Kabbalah Unveiled Knorr von Islam

The Qur’an in a month All hundred and fourteen suras in thirty days, in Pickthall’s translation of 1930 — roughly the pace of a juz a night in Ramadan. 29 days · 114 readings The Qur’an, a sura a day All hundred and fourteen suras in Pickthall’s translation, one a day, in the order of the book — the long Medinan suras first, the short Meccan ones at the end. 114 days · 114 readings The Qur’an in two voices Sixty days through the whole Qur’an, each day’s suras read twice: once in Pickthall’s translation of 1930 and once in George Sale’s of 1734. Where the two Englishmen disagree, the Arabic is usually doing something interesting. 60 days · 228 readings Juz’ Amma The last thirtieth of the Qur’an — the short Meccan suras that children learn first — one a day, from An-Naba to An-Nas. 37 days · 37 readings The prophets in the Qur’an Eighteen days through the Qur’an’s prophets in their order — Adam, Noah, the Arabian prophets, Abraham, Lot, Joseph, Moses, Khidr, David and Solomon, Job and Jonah, Elias, Zechariah and John, Mary and Jesus, and the Seal — each with the Bible chapter that tells the same story. 18 days · 56 readings Sale’s Qur’an in a month The first English Qur’an made from the Arabic, George Sale’s of 1734, in thirty days. 30 days · 114 readings The Life of Mohammad Étienne Dinet and Sliman ben Ibrahim’s life of the Prophet, written by a French convert and his Algerian friend, a chapter a day. 16 days · 16 readings Mahomet, Founder of Islam Gladys Draycott’s life of the Prophet, from Mecca to the last rites, a chapter a day. 21 days · 21 readings The Confessions of al-Ghazali The Deliverance from Error — how the greatest theologian of Islam lost his certainty, gave up his chair and found it again among the Sufis — in nine days. 9 days · 9 readings Letters from a Sufi teacher Sharafuddin Maneri’s fourteenth-century letters to a disciple — on unity, conversion, the stages of the path — two a day for a month. 30 days · 60 readings Rumi Hadland Davis’s introduction to Jalal ad-Din Rumi and the early Sufis, with selections from the Masnavi and the Divan, two sections a day. 21 days · 41 readings Sayings of the Prophet Muhammad A collection of the Prophet’s sayings translated into English, a section a day — the Hadith in its early, narrative form. 120 days · 120 readings The Kashf al-Mahjub The oldest Persian treatise on Sufism, Ali ibn Uthman al-Hujwiri’s Unveiling of the Veiled, a section a day. 160 days · 160 readings The Sufistic Quatrains of Omar Khayyam The quatrains read as the Sufi poetry their translator took them to be, a page a day. 80 days · 159 readings The Kitáb-i-Aqdas Bahá’u’lláh’s Most Holy Book, the charter of the Bahá’í faith, a section a day. 111 days · 111 readings Gleanings from Bahá’u’lláh Selections from the writings of the founder of the Bahá’í faith, gathered by Shoghi Effendi, a section a day. 11 days · 11 readings Sale’s Koran, illustrated The George Sale translation reissued with full commentary and explanatory notes, in its own thirty-day shape. 29 days · 114 readings

Across the scriptures

Beginnings, three ways Fourteen days on the same handful of stories — the creation, the flood, Abraham, Joseph, Moses — read in the Hebrew, Christian and Muslim scriptures one after another, so the differences are in front of you rather than in memory. 14 days · 33 readings In the beginning Ten days on the first two chapters of Genesis and everything that was built on them: the psalms and Job, the Qur’an’s six days, Philo, the midrash, the rabbis’ day-by-day of creation, Maimonides, and the new creation at the end of the Bible. 10 days · 69 readings Noah and the flood A week on the flood: Genesis, the sura named for Noah, Jubilees, the Legends of the Jews, the New Testament’s rereading, the midrash, and Philo. 7 days · 34 readings Abraham, three ways Ten days with the man all three traditions call father: Genesis and the Legends of the Jews side by side, the Qur’an’s Ibrahim, Paul and James on his faith, the binding of Isaac in Genesis, the Qur’an, the midrash and Rabbi Eliezer, and Maimonides’ account of how Abraham worked out for himself that there is one God. 10 days · 44 readings Joseph and his brothers Genesis 37–50 a few chapters a day, each day paired with the Legends of the Jews retelling the same scenes; then the twelfth sura, “the best of stories”, in two translations; then Philo’s Joseph and the way the later books remembered him. 10 days · 26 readings Moses, three ways Twelve days from the Nile to Mount Nebo: Exodus, Numbers and Deuteronomy against the suras that tell the same story, with Stephen’s speech, the Passover in the Mishnah and Maimonides, the Legends of the Jews, and Maimonides on why Moses’ prophecy was unlike any other. 12 days · 60 readings The Ten Words The commandments at Sinai, twice in the Torah and once in the Qur’an; Philo’s treatise on them across four days; Jesus, Paul and Luther on what they ask; and the Sefer HaChinukh counting them one by one. 10 days · 61 readings The Sermon on the Mount and its neighbours Matthew 5–7 a chapter a day and then a week of what stands beside it: the Ten Words, the Fatiha and Ayat al-Kursi, Pirkei Avot, Leviticus 19, the psalms of the blessed, Proverbs on feeding your enemy, the two ways, and the greatest commandment. 10 days · 42 readings Hear, O Israel Eight days on the oneness of God: the Shema and its paragraphs, Maimonides’ laws of reading it, Isaiah’s “there is none else”, the Qur’an’s Ikhlas and Throne Verse, Paul and James, Halevi’s Kuzari and Bahya’s Gate of Unity. 8 days · 38 readings Prayer, three ways Ten days of prayer as each tradition taught it: the psalms, the Lord’s Prayer and the Fatiha, the Mishnah and Maimonides on the Shema and the Amidah, the night prayer of the Qur’an, Solomon’s, Daniel’s and Ezra’s prayers, and Ibn Gabirol’s Keter Malkhut across three days. 10 days · 76 readings Wisdom, three ways A fortnight of the wisdom books and their cousins: Proverbs and Luqman, Sirach and the Wisdom of Solomon, Ecclesiastes and the parable of the two gardens, Pirkei Avot and James, Ibn Gabirol’s Choice of Pearls, Solomon and Sheba, and Maimonides’ Eight Chapters. 14 days · 67 readings The poor at the gate Eight days on what the three scriptures ask for the poor: the tithe and the gleaning, the Qur’an’s alms, Isaiah and Amos, Lazarus at the gate, Paul’s collection, Tobit’s charge to his son, and Maimonides’ eight degrees of charity. 8 days · 35 readings Return: a month on repentance Thirty days for the season of turning. The prophets and psalms of return, the Qur’an on forgiveness, the lost son and the publican, then Maimonides’ ten chapters on repentance one a day with a psalm each, Rabbeinu Yonah’s Gates of Repentance, Rav Kook’s Lights of Repentance, and the Day of Atonement itself. 30 days · 64 readings Job, and what to say to the suffering The whole of Job in a fortnight, three chapters a day, opened with the Qur’an’s Ayyub and closed with the way James, Ezekiel, Tobit and the Talmud handled him. 16 days · 49 readings Mary and the child Six days on the birth of Jesus as Luke, Matthew and John tell it and as the Qur’an tells it in the suras of the Family of Imran and of Mary, with the Isaiah, Micah and Numbers they all reach back to. 6 days · 26 readings Jonah, four ways Four days, one for each chapter of Jonah, with the Qur’an’s Yunus, the Legends of the Jews, Rabbi Eliezer’s chapter on the fish, and the sign of Jonah in the Gospels. 4 days · 12 readings David and Solomon Twelve days from the anointing to the queen of Sheba: Samuel and Kings, the psalms attached to each episode, the Legends of the Jews, and the Qur’an’s Dawud and Sulayman. 12 days · 40 readings Elijah A week with the prophet who did not die: Kings, Sirach’s praise of him, the Qur’an’s Ilyas, the Gospels’ Elijah, and the Legends of the Jews on what he has been doing since. 7 days · 15 readings Women of the three scriptures A fortnight: Eve, Sarah and Hagar, Rebekah, Rachel and Leah, Tamar and Zuleika, Miriam, Deborah, Ruth, Hannah, the queen of Sheba, Esther, Judith and Susanna, and Mary — each in the scripture that tells her story and in the Legends of the Jews, the midrash and the Qur’an. 14 days · 61 readings The end of days A fortnight on the last things: Daniel’s visions and the suras of the Overthrowing and the Earthquake, Isaiah’s mountain and Ezekiel’s dry bones, Gog and Magog, Elijah’s return, the Gospels’ little apocalypse, Paul, Revelation, Maimonides on the Messiah, and Esdras and Wisdom on the world to come. 14 days · 55 readings Death and what follows Twelve days on mortality and the hope beyond it: Genesis and Ecclesiastes, Job and the psalms, the Qur’an’s Ya Sin and Al-Waqi‘a, Maccabees and Wisdom, Lazarus and Paul on the resurrection, the Mishnah’s share in the world to come, Maimonides’ laws of mourning, Nahmanides’ Gate of Reward, and Yizkor. 12 days · 48 readings
