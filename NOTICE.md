# What is free here, and what is not

This project holds two different things, and they are not under the same terms.

## The texts — public domain

Every source text in this library is in the public domain, and every modernized
edition of one made here is offered on the same terms: **free to read, copy,
print, sell and give away, by anyone, for any purpose, without permission or
attribution.**

That is the point of the library and it is not a side effect. Texts were chosen
against that standard and rejected when they failed it — a licence that forbids
commercial use is not free enough for this library, however good the
translation, and neither is a publisher's word that a 1941 copyright has
expired when the date on the cover says otherwise.

If you want the texts, take them.

## The software — MIT

The code that makes this library — the packing and build tools, the JavaScript
that draws the pages, the Python that writes them — is released under the
**MIT License**. The full text is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

You may use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense and sell it,
provided the copyright notice and the licence travel with it. It comes with no
warranty.

## Third-party components

- The **Gentium fonts** in `fonts/` are used under the SIL Open Font License;
  their licence travels with them in `fonts/OFL.txt` and applies to them alone.
- The **Strong's dictionaries**, the **Hebrew interlinear** and the **Greek
  interlinear** are used under CC BY-SA and CC BY 4.0 respectively; the credit
  they require is on the site's `about.html`, under "Sources and rights".
