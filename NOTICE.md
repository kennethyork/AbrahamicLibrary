# What is free here, and what is not

This project holds two different things, and they are not under the same terms.

## The texts — free

Every source text in this archive is in the public domain, and every modernized
edition of one made here is offered on the same terms: **free to read, copy,
print, sell and give away, by anyone, for any purpose, without permission or
attribution.**

That is the point of the archive and it is not a side effect. Texts were chosen
against that standard and rejected when they failed it — a licence that forbids
commercial use is not free enough for this library, however good the
translation, and neither is a publisher's word that a 1941 copyright has
expired when the date on the cover says otherwise.

If you want the texts, take them.

## The software — not free

The code in this repository — `app/`, `tools/`, `bible/`, and the build
scripts — is **copyright the author, all rights reserved.** It is not open
source. No licence to copy, modify, redistribute or run it is granted by its
presence here.

The absence of a `LICENSE` file is deliberate: under copyright law the default
is that these rights are reserved, and they are.

## Third-party components

The Gentium fonts in `fonts/` are used under the SIL Open Font License; their
licence travels with them in `fonts/OFL.txt` and applies to them alone.
