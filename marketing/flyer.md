### What's inside (two columns)

**The Deuterocanonical Books**
Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch, 1 and 2 Maccabees, and the additions
to Esther and Daniel.

**The Wider Apocrypha**
1 and 2 Esdras, the Prayer of Manasseh, Psalm 151, 3 and 4 Maccabees.

**The Book of Enoch** — entire.

**The Pseudepigrapha**
Adam and Eve, the Secrets of Enoch, the Psalms and Odes of Solomon, Aristeas,
Ahikar, the Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs.

**The New Testament Apocrypha**
The Protevangelion, the infancy gospels, Nicodemus, Laodiceans, Paul and
Thecla, Paul and Seneca.

**The Apostolic Fathers**
The Didache, Clement, Barnabas, Ignatius, Polycarp, Diognetus, the Shepherd
of Hermas, Papias.

### Why this one (three short blocks)

**One English, not five.** Five public-domain sources spanning three centuries,
brought to one modern American standard. 805 corrections, each checked in
place. The translators' word order untouched.

**Written to be entered.** Twenty-two of the harder books open with a page of
plain-language orientation. A summary of all sixty-six books of the common
canon closes the volume, cross-referenced to the works printed here.

**Made to be read.** Two columns, footnotes at the foot of the column that
holds them, a binding margin wide enough for a book this thick. Set in
Gentium Plus.
