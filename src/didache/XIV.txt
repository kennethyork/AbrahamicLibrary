1. But every Lord&#8217;s day<ref>

Comp. Rev. i. 10. Here the full form is &#954;&#945;&#964;&#8048; &#954;&#965;&#961;&#953;&#945;&#954;&#8052;&#957; &#948;&#8050; &#922;&#965;&#961;&#8055;&#959;&#957;. If the early date is allowed, this verse confirms the view that from the first the Lord&#8217;s Day was observed, and that, too, by a eucharistic celebration.  &#160;</ref> do ye gather yourselves together, and break bread, and give thanksgiving after having confessed your transgressions,<ref>

On this spiritual sense of &#8220;sacrifice,&#8221; comp. Rom. xii. 1; Phil. ii. 17; Heb. xiii. 15; 1 Pet. ii. 5.  &#160;</ref> 2. But let no one that is at variance<ref>
